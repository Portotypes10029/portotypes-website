About Us

We’re a veteran robotics team founded in 2025 at Portage High School in Portage, Indiana. As part of FIRST Indiana Robotics, we compete in district events across the state. Our team is made up of passionate high school students who love designing, building, and programming competitive robots. Our mission is to learn, innovate, and challenge ourselves to compete at the highest levels of robotics.
